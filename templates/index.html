⚖️ Mini Courtroom

Litigation-prep simulator — paste one case, get an evidence matrix, verified legal sources, both sides argued, a Judge's critique, and a cited case brief. It stress-tests arguments and finds weaknesses; it does not decide cases. LLM: {{ llm_provider }}.

Not legal advice. This is a simulation for legal education and litigation preparation. It is not a real court judgment and does not predict how a real court would rule. A qualified lawyer must review and verify everything.
● Evidence AnalystParses the statement into an evidence matrix first — Reported, Unverified or Unsupported — before anyone argues.
● Legal ResearchRetrieves only verified Indian statutes and judgments. No source, no citation — the claim is labelled unsupported.
● Claimant's CounselArgues the claim from the evidence matrix and verified sources only.
● Respondent's CounselArgues the other side — burden of proof, gaps, unsupported claims.
● Judge — critique, not a verdictIdentifies unsupported claims, contradictions and evidence gaps, and gives a preliminary assessment.
● Case BriefA structured, downloadable brief a lawyer can review: facts, evidence, law, weaknesses, missing evidence.

Each line becomes a row in the evidence matrix, labelled as a user-supplied note — reported as you stated it, not verified against the original document. Leave it empty to analyse the case statement alone.
