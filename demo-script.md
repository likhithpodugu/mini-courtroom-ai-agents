# Demo script — Mini Courtroom (Litigation-Prep Simulator)

**Length:** ~90 seconds spoken. **Case:** the rent-deposit sample
(Priya Sharma vs Ramesh Kumar, ₹50,000 deposit, ₹20,000 repair
deduction with no bills). Record on your phone, screen-recording the
live demo at https://mini-courtroom-ai-agents.onrender.com — open
the link a minute early, because the free tier can take ~50 seconds
to wake up after idle.

## Clip 1 — The case, in one screen (0:00–0:15)

> "This is Mini Courtroom, a litigation-prep simulator. A tenant,
> Priya Sharma, paid a ₹50,000 deposit. Her landlord refused to
> refund it, claiming ₹20,000 for repairs — with no bills. I paste
> that one case statement, and I can also paste evidence excerpts,
> like Clause 4 of the rental agreement. Then I click Analyse."

*On screen: paste the rent-deposit sample, add the Clause 4 note in
the evidence field, click "Analyse the case".*

## Clip 2 — Analyse and Research (0:15–0:40)

> "First, the Evidence Analyst builds a matrix before anyone argues.
> The deposit, the agreement, the photos — Reported. But the
> ₹20,000 repair claim is flagged Unsupported: no bills or receipts
> supplied. Then Legal Research retrieves only verified Indian
> sources — the Contract Act, the Transfer of Property Act, and
> Supreme Court decisions like Kailash Nath — with links. If no
> source supports a claim, it is labelled unsupported, never
> invented."

*On screen: scroll the evidence matrix (point at the red
Unsupported badge), then the legal authorities panel.*

## Clip 3 — Debate and Challenge (0:40–1:05)

> "Both counsel argue from that same matrix and those same sources —
> not from free invention. And the Judge does not decide the case.
> It critiques: it calls out the unsupported ₹20,000 deduction,
> names the missing evidence, and gives a preliminary assessment —
> Mixed, partly supported — with what would change it."

*On screen: the Judge's critique panel — assessment label,
weaknesses, missing evidence. Briefly open the collapsible debate
transcript.*

## Clip 4 — Deliver (1:05–1:30)

> "The output is a case brief a lawyer can actually review — facts,
> the evidence matrix, verified law, both sides' strongest
> arguments, weaknesses, and the questions to ask the client next.
> I download it as Markdown. This is preparation, not a verdict —
> and not legal advice."

*On screen: click "Download case brief (.md)", show the brief's
headings. End on the disclaimer line.*

## Phone recording tips

- Record in a quiet room, phone on a stand, landscape.
- Screen-record the demo; record your voice over it after, so a
  slow page load does not ruin a take.
- Do one take per clip — a pause between clips is fine, they cut
  together.
- If the live page is slow to wake, wait for it fully before
  starting clip 1; never record the loading spinner.
